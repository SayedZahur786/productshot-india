# Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

## Who operates this service
ProductShot India is an early software preview maintained by Sayed Zahur. Use the studio’s Delete my images & account control for self-service deletion. The maintainer’s public profile is https://github.com/SayedZahur786. Do not post private images, payment details or personal data publicly. A private support address and verified merchant identity are required before paid launch.

## Information and purpose
The studio uses product photos you choose, generated cutouts and finished images to prepare your requested exports. Background segmentation runs in your browser. When cloud processing is enabled, the original and cutout are sent to our server for validation and formatting; originals and finished files are stored privately. A random browser session identifies your image balance and ownership. We do not request an email address or use your images to train models.

## Cookies and analytics
An essential HTTP-only session cookie keeps your credits and downloads tied to this browser for up to 30 days. Clearing it loses access; there is no account recovery in this preview. Optional analytics are disabled. The shared analytics component sends nothing without explicit consent and respects Do Not Track. Payment-provider cookies may apply on the provider’s own checkout site when payments become available.

## Service providers and transfers
The application runs on Vercel with Neon Postgres and private Neon S3-compatible object storage. Background-removal model files are hosted with the app on Vercel; segmentation happens in your browser. Razorpay will process payment details on its own checkout when merchant setup is complete. Providers can process data outside India; this preview makes no data-residency or regulatory certification claim.

## Retention and security
Cloud download authorization expires after 24 hours, and individual signed object links expire after 60 seconds. A daily cleanup removes expired cloud objects, normally within 48 hours of upload; failed cleanup jobs require operator retry. Local development files stay on the developer machine until deletion. Account and credit records persist until you delete the browser account. Rate-limit counters use one-hour windows. HTTP-only cookies, ownership checks and bounded image decoding reduce risk; no system can promise perfect security.

## Your choices and rights
Use Delete my images & account in the studio to request deletion of your stored images and account, while the session is still available. Download desired files first. Browser object previews are released when the studio closes. You can refuse uploads and browse samples without processing personal photos. If you lose your session, consult the maintainer profile without sharing sensitive data publicly; a dedicated private support route is not available in this preview. Legal data rights depend on your circumstances and applicable law.

## Changes
We will update this policy and its effective date as infrastructure or data practices change. Paid processing will not open until the operator, private support route and retention setup are verified. Review this page before uploading commercially sensitive images.
